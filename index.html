About Bestway Medical Clinic

Bestway Medical Clinic is a WAFID-approved medical center specializing in comprehensive medical screening and diagnostics. We serve international patients requiring medical examinations for visa applications, work permits, and residency documentation with professional care and accurate assessments.

International Standards

WAFID-approved facility meeting international medical screening standards for visa and work permit applications.

Quick Processing

Services

Visa Medical Screening

Complete medical examination for visa applications including physical assessment, laboratory tests, and X-ray screening as required by immigration authorities.

Work Permit Medical

Occupational health screening and medical assessment for employment abroad and work permit applications.

Imaging Services
